# Budgetto finance UX
- Monetary values: preserve exact precision; format locale and currency explicitly.
- Distinguish balance, income, expense, transfer, and forecast visually and in text. Do not use color alone.
- Forecasts and AI advice must disclose assumptions, source timeframe, and uncertainty. Never present inferred results as bank-confirmed facts.
- Bank imports: show file/source, imported count, duplicates, errors and user review before applying mutations.
- Offer reversible edits and clear audit trail for financial records.
- Privacy: never show secrets or tokens; redact sensitive data from analytics and diagnostics.
- Responsive dashboards must prioritize current balances, recent transactions and actionable alerts rather than decorative charts.
